Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Level Tool by Albara Hassan

The short version: Level Tool has no account, no ads and no tracking. Everything you create stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Motion data. Level Tool reads your iPhone’s motion sensors on your iPhone only, to show the live angle. Readings are never stored or sent anywhere, except a measurement you choose to save.
  • Saved measurements and settings. Measurements you name and save, your target angle and your calibration are stored only on your iPhone.

What leaves your iPhone

  • Purchases (Apple). Payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • Subscription status (RevenueCat). We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase receipt, a random anonymous ID created by the app, and basic device information such as the app version and country. It does not receive anything you create in the app. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

That’s the complete list. Level Tool doesn’t use advertising, third-party analytics or tracking, and doesn’t sell or share your data.

Usage events

To understand whether the app is useful, Level Tool records a few simple events (for example “first result shown” or “paywall viewed”) in a small file on your iPhone. These events are never sent anywhere.

Children

Level Tool is a general-audience app and isn’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone. To cancel a subscription, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
