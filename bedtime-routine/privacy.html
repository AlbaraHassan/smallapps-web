Bedtime Routine

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Bedtime Routine by Albara Hassan

The short version: Bedtime Routine has no account, no ads and no tracking. Everything you create stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your routines. Routine names, steps and tonight’s progress are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Your reminder times. Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your iPhone itself. Reminder times never leave the device.

What leaves your iPhone

  • Purchases (Apple). Payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • Subscription status (RevenueCat). We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase receipt, a random anonymous ID created by the app, and basic device information such as the app version and country. It does not receive anything you create in the app. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

That’s the complete list. Bedtime Routine doesn’t use advertising, third-party analytics or tracking, and doesn’t sell or share your data.

Usage events

To understand whether the app is useful, Bedtime Routine records a few simple events (for example “first result shown” or “paywall viewed”) in a small file on your iPhone. These events are never sent anywhere.

Children

Bedtime Routine is made for parents and caregivers. It isn’t directed at children, and it doesn’t collect any information about children or anyone else. Step names you type stay on your iPhone.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone. To cancel a subscription, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
