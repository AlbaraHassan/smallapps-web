Support

Need help with Blind Ranking: Party Challenge? Email editestweb@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you, usually within two business days.

Restore a purchase

Open Blind Ranking: Party Challenge, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Cancel a subscription

Open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Blind Ranking: Party Challenge › Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period.

Support ID

In Settings you can copy an anonymous Support ID. Include it in your email only if you want us to look up your subscription. Please don’t send your project content unless it’s needed.

Back up or delete your data

Settings has Export for a backup copy and Delete All Data. Deleting app data doesn’t cancel a subscription or remove exported files or device backups.
