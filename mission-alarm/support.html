Restore a purchase

Open Mission Alarm, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Cancel a subscription

Open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Mission Alarm › Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the paid period.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
