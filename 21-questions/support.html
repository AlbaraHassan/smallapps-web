Restore a purchase

Open 21 Questions, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Cancel a subscription

Open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › 21 Questions › Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the paid period.
